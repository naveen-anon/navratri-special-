Nau Roop, Nau Raat

Card kholo: mantra, kahani aur bhog. Aaj ki raat highlighted hai.

Vrat ke liye phalahar

  • Kuttu ya singhade ke atte ki puri
  • Sabudana khichdi, samak ki kheer
  • Aloo jeera, lauki, kaddu ki sabzi
  • Dahi, makhana, fruits
  • Sirf sendha namak

Ghar ki puja

  • Pratipada par kalash sthapana
  • Jau boyein, roz pani dein
  • Subah shaam aarti
  • Ashtami ya Navami par kanya pujan
  • Dashami par visarjan

Muhurat apne local panchang se confirm karo.

Garba basics

  • Teen taali: dono haath se 3 taali, phir ghoomo
  • Gol ghere me Maa ki murti ya diya beech me
  • Seedha chalo, anti-clockwise circle

Dandiya basics

  • Do sticks, partner ke saath tap
  • Pehle apni sticks, phir partner ki
  • Beat tez hoti jaaye to step chhote rakho
